What is Import Export Registration?

Import Export Registration is a mandatory process for businesses engaged in international trade. It includes obtaining IEC (Import Export Code) from DGFT and other necessary registrations to ensure smooth import and export operations.

DGFTDirectorate General of Foreign Trade

Required Documents

1. Proprietorship
  1. 1. PAN Card Copy
  2. 2. Aadhaar Card Copy
  3. 3. Company Name and Address
  4. 4. Mobile Number and Email Address
  5. 5. Main Objective of Business
  6. 6. Cancelled Cheque Leaf (Company)

  1. 1. Company PAN Card
  2. 2. Partnership Deed
