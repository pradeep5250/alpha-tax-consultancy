Documents Required (SPICE BOARD LICENCE)

Proprietorship

  • 1. PAN Card Copy
  • 2. Aadhaar Card Copy (Proprietor)
  • 3. Business Name and Address Proof
  • 4. Mobile Number and Email Address
  • 5. Main Objective of Business
  • 6. Bank Cancelled Cheque
  • 7. MSME Certificate (if available)

Partnership Firm

  • 1. PAN Card of Firm
  • 2. Partnership Deed
  • 3. PAN Card of All Partners
  • 4. Aadhaar Card of All Partners
  • 5. Business Address Proof
  • 6. Mobile Number and Email
  • 7. Main Objective of Business
  • 8. Bank Cancelled Cheque
  • 9. MSME Certificate

Private Limited Company

  • 1. PAN Card of Company
  • 2. Certificate of Incorporation
  • 3. MOA & AOA
  • 4. PAN Card of Directors
  • 5. Aadhaar Card of Directors
  • 6. Registered Office Address Proof
  • 7. Mobile Number and Email
  • 8. Main Objective of Business
  • 9. Bank Cancelled Cheque
  • 10. MSME Certificate
