Required Documents

1. Proprietorship
  1. 1. PAN Card Copy
  2. 2. Aadhaar Card Copy
  3. 3. Business / Shop Address Proof (Rent Agreement / Property Document)
  4. 4. Latest Electricity Bill / Water Bill / Property Tax Receipt
  5. 5. Shop / Establishment Photos (Front & Inside)
  6. 6. Mobile Number & Email Address
  7. 7. Nature of Business / Activity Details
  8. 8. NOC from Owner (if Rented Property)
2. Partnership Firm
  1. 1. Partnership Deed
  2. 2. Company PAN Card
  3. 3. Partners PAN Cards
  4. 4. Partners Aadhaar Cards
  5. 5. Business / Shop Address Proof (Rent Agreement / Property Document)
  6. 6. Latest Electricity Bill / Water Bill / Property Tax Receipt
  7. 7. Shop / Establishment Photos (Front & Inside)
  8. 8. Mobile Number & Email Address
  9. 9. Nature of Business / Activity Details
  10. 10. NOC from Owner (if Rented Property)
3. Private Limited Company
  1. 1. Certificate of Incorporation
  2. 2. Company PAN Card
  3. 3. MOA & AOA
  4. 4. Directors PAN Cards
  5. 5. Directors Aadhaar Cards
  6. 6. Business / Registered Office Address Proof (Rent Agreement / Property Document)
  7. 7. Latest Electricity Bill / Water Bill / Property Tax Receipt
  8. 8. Shop / Establishment Photos (Front & Inside)
  9. 9. Mobile Number & Email Address
  10. 10. Nature of Business / Activity Details
  11. 11. NOC from Owner (if Rented Property)
  12. 12. Board Resolution (if Applicable)
