PROPRIETORSHIP
  1. 1. PAN Card Copy
  2. 2. Aadhaar Card Copy
  3. 3. Company Name and Address
  4. 4. Mobile Number and Email Address
  5. 5. Bank Statement / Cancelled Cheque
  6. 6. Photograph of Proprietor
  7. 7. Business Place Proof (Rent / Ownership)

PARTNERSHIP FIRM
