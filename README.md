## 📌 Overview

**Alpha Tax Consultancy** is an all-in-one legal-tech and financial advisory portal providing business owners, startups, NGOs, and corporations with streamlined registration, tax filing, and regulatory compliance. The platform features an interactive quote generator, customized service calculators, and dedicated information pages covering over 30 distinct statutory services.

---

## ✨ Covered Statutory & Legal Services

### 🏢 Business Incorporation & Structuring
- **Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd)**
- **Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)**
- **One Person Company (OPC)**
- **Partnership Firm & Sole Proprietorship**
- **Public Limited Company**
- **Section 8 Company (Non-Profit NGO)**

### 📑 Taxation & Regulatory Filings
- **GST Registration & Monthly GSTR Filing**
- **Income Tax Return (ITR) Filing (Individuals & Corporate)**
- **TDS Compliance & Returns**
- **ROC (Registrar of Companies) Annual Filings**
- **PF & ESI Employee Registration & Remittance**
- **Professional Tax (PT) State Registration**

### 📜 Licensing, Certifications & NGO Approvals
- **80G & 12A Income Tax Exemption Registration**
- **NGO NITI Aayog Darpan Registration**
- **FSSAI Food Business Licensing**
- **APEDA & Spice Board Export Licensing**
- **Import Export Code (IEC)**
- **ISO 9001/14001/27001 Certification Assistance**
- **Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) Code**
- **Class 3 Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)**
- **Trademark Search, Filing & Protection**
- **Shops & Establishment Trade License**

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Structure:** Semantic HTML5 with accessibility standards
- **Styling:** CSS3, Flexbox, CSS Grid, Custom animations (`animations.css`, `styles.css`)
- **Interactivity:** Vanilla JavaScript (`animations.js`, `quote-modal.js`, `script.js`)
- **Interactive Modals:** Instant quotation estimator and enquiry modal
