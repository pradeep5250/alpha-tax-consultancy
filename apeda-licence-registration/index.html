Documents Required (APEDA LICENCE)

Proprietorship

  • 1. PAN Card
  • 2. Aadhaar Card
  • 3. Business Name / Address Proof
  • 4. Mobile Number & Email
  • 5. Main Objective of Business
  • 6. Bank Cancelled Cheque
  • 7. MSME Certificate

Partnership Firm

  • 1. PAN of Firm
  • 2. Partnership Deed
  • 3. PAN of Partners
  • 4. Aadhaar of Partners
  • 5. Business Address Proof
  • 6. Mobile Number & Email
  • 7. Main Objective of Business
  • 8. Bank Cancelled Cheque
  • 9. MSME Certificate

Private Limited Company

  • 1. PAN of Company
  • 2. Certificate of Incorporation
  • 3. MOA & AOA
  • 4. PAN of Directors
  • 5. Aadhaar of Directors
  • 6. Registered Office Proof
  • 7. Mobile Number & Email
  • 8. Main Objective of Business
  • 9. Bank Cancelled Cheque
  • 10. MSME Certificate
