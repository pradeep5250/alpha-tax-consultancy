Why Choose a Public Limited Company?

Separate Legal Entity

The company is a separate legal entity from its members.

Raise Capital

Easily raise funds through public issue of shares.

High Credibility

Enhances business reputation and builds trust among investors.

Perpetual Succession

Business continues even with the change in shareholders.

Limited Liability

Shareholders' liability is limited to the extent of their shareholding.

Global Opportunities

Expand globally and enter into large scale business operations.

Documents Required
  1. 1. PAN Card Copy
  2. 2. ID Proof (Aadhaar / DL / Voter ID)
  3. 3. Address Proof (Bank Statement / EB / Mobile Bill)
  4. 4. Passport Size Photos
  5. 5. Proof of Registered Office
  6. 6. Mobile Number / Email Address
  7. 7. Company Name and Address
  8. 8. Separate Mobile / Email for Company
  9. 9. Main Objective of Business
Minimum Requirements

Minimum 7 Shareholders

At least 7 members are required to form a Public Limited Company.

Minimum 3 Directors

At least 3 Directors are required to form a Public Limited Company.
