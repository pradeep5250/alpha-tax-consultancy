Documents Required
  1. 1. PAN Card Copy
  2. 2. Aadhaar Card / ID Proof of Proprietor / Partners / Directors
  3. 3. Address Proof of Business Place (Rent Agreement / Property Document / Latest Utility Bill)
  4. 4. Passport Size Photograph
  5. 5. Nature of Business / Business Activity Details
  6. 6. Employee Details (if any)
  7. 7. Contact Number & Email ID
  8. 8. If Partnership Firm – Partnership Deed
  9. 9. If Company – Incorporation Certificate
