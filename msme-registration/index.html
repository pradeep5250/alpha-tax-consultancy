Who Can Apply?

Proprietorship

Individuals carrying on business in their own name.

Partnership Firm

Registered partnership firms under the Partnership Act.

Private Limited Company

Private limited companies registered under the Companies Act, 2013.

Documents Required

Proprietorship

  1. 1. PAN Card Copy
  2. 2. Aadhaar Card Copy
  3. 3. Bank A/c Details
  4. 4. Company Name and Address
  5. 5. Mobile Number and Email Address
  6. 6. Main Objective of Business

Partnership Firm

  1. 1. Company PAN Card
  2. 2. Partnership Deed
  3. 3. Partners Aadhaar Card Copy
  4. 4. Bank A/c Details
  5. 5. Company Address
  6. 6. Mobile Number and Email Address
  7. 7. Main Objective of Business

Private Limited Company

  1. 1. Company PAN Card
  2. 2. Incorporation Certificate
  3. 3. Directors Aadhaar Card Copy
  4. 4. Bank A/c Details
  5. 5. Company Address
  6. 6. Mobile Number and Email Address
  7. 7. Main Objective of Business
