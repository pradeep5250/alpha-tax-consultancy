Why Choose LLP?

Limited Liability

Partners are not personally liable for the debts or losses of the LLP.

Separate Legal Entity

LLP has a separate legal identity from its partners.

Flexible Management

Design your own agreement with maximum flexibility in operations.

Cost Effective

Lower compliance cost compared to Private Limited Company.

Easy Compliance

Simplified compliance requirements and fewer regulatory burdens.

Documents Required
  1. 1. PAN Card Copy
  2. 2. ID Proof (Aadhaar / DL / Voter ID)
  3. 3. Address Proof (Bank Statement / EB / Mobile Bill)
  4. 4. Passport Size Photographs
  5. 5. Proof of Registered Office
  6. 6. Mobile Number / Email Address
  7. 7. Proposed LLP Name
  8. 8. Partners' Details & Contribution
  9. 9. Main Objective of Business
Minimum Requirement

Minimum 2 Partners

To register an LLP, a minimum of 2 partners are required.
