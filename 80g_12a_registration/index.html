Benefits of 12A & 80G Registration

Tax Exemption (12A)

Income of the Trust / NGO is exempt from tax, subject to conditions.

Tax Deduction for Donors (80G)

Donors can claim deduction on their donations, encouraging more contributions.

Increased Credibility

Registered NGOs are more trusted by donors, corporates, and government bodies.

Easy Fundraising

Documents Required

For 12A Registration
  1. 1. Trust Deed / Registration Certificate
  2. 2. PAN Card of the Trust / NGO
  3. 3. 12A Registration Application (Form 10A)
  4. 4. Details of Trustees / Governing Body
  5. 5. Activities and Objectives of the Trust
  6. 6. Financial Statements (Last 3 Years, if applicable)
  7. 7. Address Proof of the Office
  8. 8. Audit Report (if applicable)
For 80G Registration
  1. 1. 12A Registration Certificate (Mandatory)
  2. 2. PAN Card of the Trust / NGO
  3. 3. 80G Registration Application (Form 10G)
  4. 4. Trust Deed / Registration Certificate
  5. 5. Details of Trustees / Governing Body
  6. 6. Details of Activities & Utilization of Funds
  7. 7. Financial Statements (Last 3 Years)
  8. 8. Audit Report (if applicable)
