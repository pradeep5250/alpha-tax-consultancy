Required Documents for Darpan Registration

Proprietorship

  1. 1. PAN Card
  2. 2. Aadhaar Card
  3. 3. Business Address Proof
  4. 4. Business Registration Proof
  5. 5. Bank Account Details (Cancelled Cheque)
  6. 6. Mobile Number
  7. 7. Email Address
  8. 8. Brief Description of Activity

Partnership Firm

  1. 1. Partnership Deed
  2. 2. Firm PAN Card
  3. 3. PAN / Aadhaar of All Partners
  4. 4. Address Proof
  5. 5. Bank Account Details
  6. 6. Mobile Number & Email
  7. 7. Brief Description of Activity

Private Limited Company

  1. 1. Certificate of Incorporation
  2. 2. Company PAN
  3. 3. MOA & AOA
  4. 4. DIN of Directors
  5. 5. PAN / Aadhaar of Directors
  6. 6. Office Address Proof
  7. 7. Bank Account Details
  8. 8. Mobile, Email & Activity Description
