Documents Required (SECTION 8 COMPANY)
  1. 1. PAN Card Copy
  2. 2. ID Proof (Aadhaar / DL / Voter ID)
  3. 3. Address Proof (Bank Statement / EB / Mobile Bill)
  4. 4. Passport Size Photographs
  5. 5. Proof of Registered Office
  6. 6. Mobile Number / Email Address
  7. 7. Company Name and Address
  8. 8. Main Objects of the Company
  9. 9. Declarations by Directors
  10. 10. DIN Number
Minimum Requirement

Minimum 2 Directors & Shareholders

A Section 8 Company must have at least 2 Directors & Shareholders.

Note: Section 8 companies are formed for charitable objects and cannot distribute profits to members. All income must be applied towards promoting the company's objectives.
