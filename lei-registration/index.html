LEI Registration

Global Identity for Your Business Transactions

LEI (Legal Entity Identifier) is a unique 20-digit code that provides a global identity to your business. It is mandatory for financial transactions, regulatory compliance and building trust in global markets.

Types of LEI Registration

LEI for Domestic Entities

For businesses registered and operating within India.

Examples: Proprietorship, Partnership, Private Limited, LLP, etc.

India
LEI for Foreign Entities

For businesses incorporated outside India but conducting financial transactions in India.

Examples: Foreign Companies, Branch Offices, Subsidiaries, etc.

Global
LEI for Branch Entities

For branches and offices of foreign entities operating in India.

Examples: Foreign Bank Branch, Foreign Company Branch, etc.

Branch

Documents Required (LEI REGISTRATION)

Proprietorship

  1. 1. PAN Card of Proprietor
  2. 2. Aadhaar Card
  3. 3. Business Address Proof (Electricity Bill / Rent Agreement)
  4. 4. Business Registration Proof
  5. 5. Mobile Number
  6. 6. Email Address
  7. 7. Brief Description of Business Activity

Partnership Firm

  1. 1. Partnership Deed
  2. 2. Firm PAN Card
  3. 3. PAN / Aadhaar of All Partners
  4. 4. Business Address Proof
  5. 5. Firm Registration Certificate
  6. 6. Mobile Number & Email
  7. 7. Brief Description of Business Activity

Private Limited Company

  1. 1. Certificate of Incorporation
  2. 2. Company PAN Card
  3. 3. MOA & AOA
  4. 4. DIN of Directors
  5. 5. PAN / Aadhaar of Directors
  6. 6. Registered Office Address Proof
  7. 7. Board Resolution
  8. 8. Mobile, Email & Business Description
